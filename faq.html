FAQ & Support

All three editions were rebuilt in 2026 for today's iPhones. Answers to the common questions are below.

Why have the reminders stopped coming?

It's a limit built into iOS: only 64 notifications can be scheduled before your device pauses an app's alerts. The fix is simple: open a reminder from time to time, tap it on the lock screen, or open one inside the app, and iOS keeps them flowing. That's all it takes.

The reminders vanish before I can read them

In Settings > Notifications > [your Remindfulness edition], choose the Persistent banner style so reminders stay on screen until you act on them.

Recent reminders also collect in Notification Center: swipe down from the top of your screen to see them.

How do I import a list of my own reminders?

On the My Reminders screen, tap the +/− button and choose Import. Paste your reminders, one per line, a column copied from a spreadsheet or a list from your notes already comes in this way, and tap Import. Duplicates of reminders you already have are skipped automatically.

How do I back up my reminders?

On the My Reminders screen, tap +/− and choose Back up. Your reminders appear as a list, one per line. Tap Share to send them to yourself by email or save them anywhere. To restore later, paste that list into Import.

How do I remove the ads?

Each app offers a one-time Remove Ads purchase. Tap OK when the upgrade alert appears, or tap Restore if you've already bought it on another device. One purchase, yours forever.

Can I read all the built-in reminders at once?

No, and it's deliberate: each practice lands with the most impact when it arrives fresh. You can always read and edit the reminders you create yourself, via the My Reminders list.

Why aren't my custom reminders coming up?

Reminders arrive randomly, so the more of your own you add, the more often yours appear among the ones that come with the app. To see only your own, select the Yours option on the Customize screen.

The app isn't behaving. What's the reset ritual?

First, back up your custom reminders (see above). Then delete the app: press and hold its icon, choose Remove App, then Delete App. Restart your iPhone. Reinstall from the App Store, open the app, allow notifications when asked, and restore your reminders via Import.
